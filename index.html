Method

  • Figures come from NASA's Planetary Fact Sheets.
  • Day is the rotation period. Year is the orbital period around the Sun.
  • Distance is the mean distance from the Sun. Light time to Neptune is that distance divided by the speed of light.
  • Moon counts are the confirmed totals at the time of writing and keep growing as new moons are found.
  • In the opening lineup every disc is drawn at true relative diameter. In the chapters all planets are shown at one size.
  • Planet surface maps: Solar System Scope, CC BY 4.0, based on NASA data.
