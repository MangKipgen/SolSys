    Method & sources
    • Figures come from NASA's Planetary Fact Sheets.
    • Day is the rotation period. Year is the orbital period around the Sun.
    • Mean temperature is at the surface, or at the 1 bar level for the giants.
    • Sunlight time is the mean distance from the Sun divided by 299,792 km/s.
    • Moon counts are confirmed moons as of 2023 and keep growing as new ones are found.
    • In the opening scale strip every disc is drawn at true relative diameter. In the chapters all planets are shown at one size. Orbit sizes are compressed so the inner planets stay visible.
    • Planet surface maps: Solar System Scope, CC BY 4.0, based on NASA data.
